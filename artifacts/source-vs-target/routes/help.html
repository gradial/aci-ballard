Help center

Short answers. If yours isn't here, call 1-800-555-0163, Monday to Friday, 7 to 5 Pacific.

Orders and shipping

In-stock gear ships in 1 to 2 business days from Kent, WA. Drops ship in 3. You'll get a tracking number by email.

Free over $99 in the US and Canada. Under that, $8 ground. Expedited is $22 and arrives in 2 business days.

We ship to Germany, France, and Japan from local stock. Duties are included at checkout.

Check the tracking link in your confirmation email, or sign in to your account.

Returns and exchanges

60 days, worn or not. Bring it to a store or mail it back with the prepaid label from your account.

Same as returns. Stores exchange on the spot if the size is in stock.

Same 60-day window. Drops can't be exchanged for a different size once the run has sold out.

Repair

Seams, zips, snaps, seam tape, reproofing, and re-knit wool on any Ballard gear, any age. Free, except reproofing at $35. Start a repair

About two weeks from the day we receive it. Store repairs are usually faster.

Accounts, saved gear, and sign-in aren't wired up in this demo. Everything else on the site works without one.

Pro purchase

Commercial fishing crews, guides, and trades get 30% off the main line with a current license or union card. Apply in any store.

Accessibility

We build to WCAG 2.2 AA. If you're using a screen reader and something isn't working, call 1-800-555-0163 and we'll take the order by phone.

Privacy and terms

This is a demo site. The bag is stored in your browser only. Nothing you type in a form is sent anywhere.

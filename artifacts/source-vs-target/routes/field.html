Programs

Uniform programs

Issued gear for crews of 50 to 5,000. Sizing runs, reorder windows, and a named account lead.

Sizing programs

On-site fit sessions for programs over 200. Sizes XS to 5XL, regular and tall, across the core line.

Embroidery and marking

Left chest, right chest, and back yoke. Digitized once, applied at our Kent, WA facility. 10 business days added.

Reorder

Core styles held in stock for 24 months from program start. Reorders ship in 10 business days.

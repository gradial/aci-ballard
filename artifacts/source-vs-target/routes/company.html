About Ballard

Gear that earns its keep.

We make gear for people whose comfort is a working condition, not a preference. The fishing fleet came first and still sets the standard.

Ballard started in 1963 as a marine outfitter in Salmon Bay, in the Ballard neighborhood of Seattle, making foul-weather gear for the North Pacific fishing fleet. We moved into technical outdoor apparel in the 1980s. The fleet is still our toughest customer, and it still tells us when something's wrong.

We sell equipment, not experiences. A jacket keeps water out. Every product page carries a spec table, a fit note, and a tradeoff line, because every product has one. If we know a limit, it's on the page.

We repair what we make, for free, for as long as it lasts. That's not a marketing program. It's how the fleet bought from us in 1963, and it's why the gear is built the way it is.

Timeline

  1. 1963Opens as a marine outfitter on Shilshole Avenue, making PVC foul-weather gear for the North Pacific fishing fleet.
  2. 1966First Deck Boot. Same last today.
  3. 1971First Salmon Bay Parka. The pattern has changed twice.
  4. 1988Moves the parka from coated cotton to a nylon shell with a PU membrane. Technical apparel becomes the main business.
  5. 2004Ballard Field starts issuing uniform programs to utilities and hospitals.
  6. 2019Ballard Drops runs its first capsule: 200 Halyards in 18 oz wax.
  7. 2025Acquires Wrenfield, a Vermont wool mill founded 1919.

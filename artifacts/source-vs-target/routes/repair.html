Repair

Free repair. Any Ballard gear, any age.

We've repaired gear since 1963 because the fleet wouldn't have bought it otherwise. Turnaround is about two weeks.

Start a repairFind a store

How it works

  1. 1

    Tell us what's wrong

    Start a request below or bring it to any store. Describe the damage in a sentence. A photo helps.

  2. 2

    Send it or drop it off

    We email a prepaid label for gear over $150. Under that, you pay shipping one way. Stores take anything.

  3. 3

    We fix it

    Seams, zips, snaps, seam tape, reproofing, re-knit cuffs and elbows. About two weeks from the day we get it.

  4. 4

    It comes back

    Return shipping is on us. If we can't fix it, we'll tell you why and offer 40% off a replacement.

What we repair

Shells and parkasSeam tape, zips, cuffs, hoods, DWR reproofing
Waxed cottonReproofing, snaps, pocket bags, collar corduroy
FleeceZips, cuffs, pocket bags
Bibs and pantsKnees, seat, suspenders, leg zips, hems
SweatersCuffs, elbows, hems, re-knit holes
Packs and duffelsZips, straps, buckles, welded seams

What we don't

Socks, gloves, and boots. Socks and gloves wear through where they're supposed to. Boots are rubber, and a patched rubber boot leaks. When these wear out, they're worn out.

Wrenfield blankets and shirts go back to the mill in Bridgewater. Any Ballard store will send them for you.

Cost

Repair is free for the life of the gear. Reproofing waxed cotton is $35 because the wax is a consumable. You pay shipping one way on gear under $150.

Built for standing on a wet deck for eight hours. 3-layer waterproof shell, 20,000mm (hydrostatic head), fully seam-sealed, with a hood that turns with your head instead of against it. Pockets sit above a harness line. Two-way front zip with a storm flap that snaps shut, so you can vent from the bottom without opening the top.

Heavy. This is not a jacket you carry in case of rain. It's one you put on when you know. If you want something packable, look at the Fathom Shell.

The tradeoff

Heavy for its warmth. You won't want to carry it in a pack.

Runs large through the shoulder. Size down if you're layering a shell over it. Length hits mid-thigh on a 5'10" wearer.
